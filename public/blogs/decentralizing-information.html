Bitcoin has been a stepping stone towards a healed society. But it shouldn't have been the first.

While bitcoin is marvellious, it should serve as an inspiration for decentralizing much more than currency. The best of which is social currency. Satoshi himself made his own forum to address bitcoin.

Why do we need a decentralized social media more than ever ?

We need to first discuss what is wrong with the current state of social media, and how wrong it can go.
The current landscape is in my opinion very different from where it needs to be.

Centralized models have proved through the Palestinian cause as a latest example, how much censorship can be made. And how much propaganda goes with it.

These are being more and more normalized, as the dopamine hit we get through our eyeballs seem more than enough to keep our subconscious minds floating.

Platforms with 2B+ users are only a handful, and they are governed by what seems like 2 to 3 people from the outside. This is more than a threat to modern-day society, as billions of people are subject to:

  • The algorithm being gamed to display what is not to their interest
  • Being censored or even better, banned for no apparent reason
  • Being shadow-banned and get way less visibility for speaking your mind
  • Scrolling through botted content and artificially boosted posts

And this is only discussing the information flow, not the digital economy that lies behind it.

You are the product

You spend your time on a platform watching content half of which is undesired

Marketers pay for ads users do not want to see

Content creators can push narratives without getting slashed for their lack of integrity

Content moderators are internal and do not moderate content based on user needs

Platform owners obey rules given by rulers and not users

What now ?

A path to a new internet

I imagine a social media where we own our content. Where we lead. A social media we as users are the proprietary of. We know what we want. We just don't know how to get it yet.

Blockchain technology showed us ways in which we can exchange currency we couldn't imagine possible 40 years ago.

Emerging technologies such as zero-knowledge proofs have shown us that it is possible to exchange information in a very private manner.

Social media is shying away from small social groups, and moving more towards a "discovery" or a "for you" page.

While very prominent as a way to interact with new accounts and to have an ever-growing social circle, this new model poses some serious threats:

You are expecting something new. Therefore, you don't know what to expect.

With this narrative, the discovery page can very well serve as a propaganda machine as the algorithm is what dictates what you see.

But in a decentralized social media, what would you see ? Who would get to be more visible ? Who doesn't ? How is the botting problem solved ?

What is needed is a social media with a social consensus. Combining zero-knowledge proofs, blockchain technology, and adding to this a consensus layer that can handle a high throughput such as hBFT, one can build a purely P2P system with a social currency at its core.

Social currency ? Another cryptocurrency ?

No. I am thinking of a proof-of-discovery-like social currency. Not a currency in a sense of getting exchanged, but a "score" that is stored on an immutable ledger like that of a blockchain. Not to counter double-spending of money, but to prove worthiness within society based on previous interactions. A currency that dictates who should be seen and who doesn't.

Think of it like a hype score. Based on your previous posts or comments, this score can go up or down based on how people see it fit with upvotes and downvotes.

Just as proof-of-stake slashes revenue for being a malicious node, this slashing mechanism could be used on the app-level among users that get to decide who is worthy to be seen through interactions.

This same slashing consensus mechanism can be at the ledger-level as well to keep nodes active. Nodes can be rewarded through discovery points, or they could be rewarded through a currency alongside the discovery points. It depends on whether or not every client is a node.

If there is a need for clients to be separate from nodes due to heavy computational needs to process the data integrity and maintain a consensus, a currency can easily be integrated into the platform without making it a pay to win situation, while maintaining a balanced platform that rewards every participant.

There will certainly be tens of different clients, each proposing a different usability of the consensus, for a different user experience. Many ideas can thrive:

  • Total elimination of bots by tipping for posting
  • Tipping on a post to reward both nodes and users for promoting specific content would be a gateway to a new form of advertising that is less boring
  • Increase in visibility through tipping for a given post, instead of growing a discovery score

TLDR; These are all random ideas that come to mind to conclude on one thing: What we are doing now is totally wrong, and does not benefit the end-user other than getting a small dopamine hit, for which the trade-off is way too big to comprehend.
